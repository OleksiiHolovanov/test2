SportyBet Responsible Gaming

Betting is meant for entertainment, not as a way to earn money or escape problems. Here is how to stay in control at SportyBet.

Bet for Fun, Stay in Control

Most people bet for fun and within what they can afford. To keep it that way, set limits before you begin and treat any spend as the price of entertainment.

Healthy Habits

  • Decide on a budget and a time limit before you start, and stick to them.
  • Never chase losses, the outcome of every match is independent.
  • Do not bet when stressed, upset or under the influence.
  • Balance betting with other activities and take regular breaks.
  • Only bet with money you can comfortably afford to lose.

Tools to Help You

SportyBet offers built-in controls to help you keep your betting in check:

  • Deposit limits: cap how much you can add over a day, week or month.
  • Session reminders: get a prompt after a set time so you stay aware of how long you have bet for.
  • Time out: take a short break by locking your account for a chosen period.
  • Self-exclusion: close access to your account for a longer period when you need distance.

To set any of these up, contact our team through Contact & Support.

Signs to Watch For

Reaching out for help may be wise if you are overspending, keeping your betting hidden, borrowing to place bets, or feeling uneasy about it. Catching these signs early really matters.

Where to Find Support

If you or someone near you needs help, confidential support is on hand from organisations focused on gambling-related harm. Our support team can also point you to the right resources whenever you ask.

Protecting Minors

You have to be 18 or older to hold an account or bet at SportyBet. We verify the age of every customer and urge parents to use parental controls that keep betting content away from children.
