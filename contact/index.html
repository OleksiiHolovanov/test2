Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things bettors ask most.

How Do I Create a SportyBet Account?

Select Register, type your mobile number with the +234 country code, set a password and confirm the SMS code. You must be 18 or over.

How Do I Log In to SportyBet?

Press the Log in button and provide the mobile number and password from your registration. Lost your password? Use the reset option on the login screen.

How Long Do Withdrawals Take?

Withdrawal times depend on your payment method and may need identity verification first. A number of bettors have noted delays at peak times, so review the cashier and current terms, and reach support if a withdrawal takes longer than expected.

How Does the SportyBet Bonus Work?

SportyBet runs promotions such as a welcome offer, accumulator boosts and free bets. Amounts and terms change over time, so check the current details on our Bonuses page before opting in.

Is There a SportyBet App?

Yes, SportyBet offers an app with the full sportsbook and live betting. See the Mobile App page, and only download from official links.

How Do I Set Limits on My Betting?

SportyBet offers responsible-gaming tools such as deposit limits and self-exclusion. Our Responsible Gaming page explains the options, and support can help you set them up.

Is SportyBet Legal in Nigeria?

Sports betting is a regulated activity in Nigeria and the rules vary across states. SportyBet operates within the regulatory frame of the markets it serves. You must be 18 or older to hold an account, and you are expected to know the rules where you bet. This is not legal advice.
