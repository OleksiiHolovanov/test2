About SportyBet

SportyBet is an online sports betting platform aimed at fans in Nigeria, with pre-match and live markets across football, basketball, tennis and virtual sports.

What SportyBet Is

SportyBet is a sports betting brand serving Nigerian bettors and a number of other African markets. The product centres on a sportsbook with pre-match and in-play markets, a fast bet slip and a mobile app that mirrors the desktop site. Football leads the line, with deep coverage of local and international fixtures, and a long tail of other sports sitting alongside.

What SportyBet Offers

  • Pre-match betting: markets opened ahead of fixtures across football, basketball, tennis, ice hockey, handball and many more.
  • Live betting: in-play markets with odds that shift as the game runs, plus cash out on selected markets.
  • Virtual sports & eSports: a permanent menu of virtual football, eFootball and other simulated events.
  • Mobile app: iOS app and Android APK that bring the sportsbook to your phone.
  • Promotions: a rolling line-up that can include welcome offers, accumulator bonuses, free bets and seasonal events.

The market list and the features on hand evolve over time. For the latest line-up, open the sportsbook itself after signing in.
