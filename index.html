SportyBet Register and Login

Opening a SportyBet account is quick. Here is the path to register, then how to sign in once the account is ready.

How to Register

  1. 1

    Tap Register

    Launch the sign-up page from the Register button at the top of the site, or from any Place a bet button.

  2. 2

    Enter Your Mobile Number

    Enter your phone number with the country code (+234 for Nigeria) and set a password. Keep the password somewhere safe.

  3. 3

    Confirm Your Account

    Type in the code sent by SMS to switch the account on. You do this just once when you first sign up.

  4. 4

    Deposit and Place a Bet

    Make a first deposit through bank transfer, card or wallet. While a welcome offer is running, you can opt in under the terms set out.

How to Log In

  1. 1

    Tap Log In

    Select the Log in button in the top right to reach the SportyBet sign-in screen.

  2. 2

    Enter Your Credentials

    Provide the phone number you registered with and your password.

  3. 3

    Stay Signed In

    Switch on Keep me signed in to skip a step next time, or reset the password from the login screen if you have lost track of it.

  4. 4

    Start Betting

    Your balance and bonuses are waiting. Pick a sport, add a market to the bet slip and stake what you are comfortable with.
